# Ethiopia legal-policy checklist

The public Privacy Policy and Terms pages are Ethiopia-oriented starter copy, not legal advice. Before launch, have qualified Ethiopian counsel review and replace their final text.

- Confirm the hotel’s legal name, licence information, physical address and policy-contact details.
- State exactly which booking, reservation, room-service, contact and newsletter data is collected; why it is collected; who can access it; and how long it is retained.
- Confirm the notices, data-subject process, security measures and other obligations applicable to the property under Ethiopia’s Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.
- Specify booking, payment, cancellation, no-show, refund and dispute-resolution terms that match the hotel’s real operations.
- Publish the approved policy text and make it easy to reach from the footer before accepting real reservations.

Reference: [Ethiopian Ministry of Justice — Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/personal-data-protection-proclamation/). Verify the current law and all sector-specific requirements before launch.
